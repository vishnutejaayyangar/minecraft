3. Minecraft beginner’s guide

Minecraft is a sandbox game by Mojang Studios in which you gather resources, craft tools, build, and survive in a world made of blocks. It was fully released in 2011 and runs on PC, consoles, and phones.

The two editions differ in some mechanics, so check which one a tutorial uses.

3.3 Your first day

  1. Punch a tree to collect wood. Gather at least 8–10 logs.
  2. Make planks from logs, then a crafting table.
  3. Craft sticks and a wooden pickaxe, then mine stone.
  4. Upgrade to stone tools (pickaxe, axe, sword) with cobblestone.
  5. Find food: cook meat from animals in a furnace, or eat apples and bread.
  6. Build a shelter before dark. Hostile mobs appear at night and in dark places. A full day lasts about 20 minutes in real time.
  7. Make torches from coal and sticks and light up your base and mines.
  8. Craft a bed (wool and planks). Sleeping at night skips to morning and sets your respawn point.
Tip: Never dig straight down. Dig stairs instead, so you can’t fall into lava or a cave.

3.5 Mobs to watch for

Zombie

Walks toward you in the dark. Easy to fight with a sword.

Skeleton

Shoots arrows from a distance. Close in fast or use cover.

Spider

Can climb walls. Dangerous at night, harmless in daylight unless attacked.

Creeper

Silently approaches and explodes. Hit it and back away, or stay out of reach.

3.6 Next goals

  • Find iron and make better tools and armor.
  • Explore caves carefully to find diamonds, which are found deep underground.
  • Build a Nether portal from obsidian and light it with flint and steel.
  • Set up a farm so food is never a problem.
  • Play with friends, either on the same world or on a server.
